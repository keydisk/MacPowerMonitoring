Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23

The app

Power Guard does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Power and battery readings are read locally from macOS, kept in memory only while the app is running, and never leave your Mac. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking and makes no network connections.
