Power Guard

Real-time power telemetry & charger safety diagnostics for MacBook.

Power Guard reads your MacBook's built-in power telemetry once per second and shows live power draw, input voltage, current and battery health. It warns you about voltage drops, adapter overload and intermittent disconnects that can point to a worn cable or a faulty charger.

Support

Requirements: macOS 13 Ventura or later, a MacBook with a built-in battery (desktop Macs don't expose battery telemetry).

Questions or bug reports: support@example.com or GitHub Issues.

FAQ

Why does it show "Cannot read battery power data"?
Your Mac has no built-in battery (Mac mini, iMac, Mac Studio, Mac Pro). Only MacBooks are supported.
Does the app collect any data?
No. All readings stay on your Mac. The app makes no network connections. See the Privacy Policy.
