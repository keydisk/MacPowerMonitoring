Mac Power Guard reads your MacBook's built-in power telemetry every second and tells you, in real time, how healthy your charger and USB-C cable really are.

Worn cables, cheap chargers and loose ports silently drop voltage and create heat. Mac Power Guard turns those hidden signals into clear numbers, live waveforms and graded warnings.

LIVE POWER DASHBOARD
• Power draw (W), input voltage (V) and current (A), updated every second
• Adapter rated wattage and voltage, plus current load
• Battery level, maximum capacity, cycle count and charging state

SAFETY EVALUATION
• Voltage drop: compares input voltage to the adapter's rating to catch rising cable resistance
• Adapter overload: tracks load against the charger's rated capacity
• Frequent disconnects: detects repeated unplug events caused by poor contact
• Telemetry errors and slow-charging limits
• Safe / Caution / Danger rating with optional alert sound

LIVE WAVEFORM CHARTS
• Last 10 min, 30 min, 1 hour, since boot, or a custom range
• LTTB downsampling keeps every spike and dip visible
• Min, max and average at a glance

PRIVACY
• Everything stays on your Mac. The app makes no network connections.
• No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Note: Requires a MacBook with a built-in battery. Mac mini, iMac, Mac Studio and Mac Pro do not expose battery power telemetry and are not supported.
This app is a diagnostic aid and does not replace certified electrical test equipment.
